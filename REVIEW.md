### Examined and Decided NOT to be Defects:
1. **Storing dates and shift times as strings (`YYYY-MM-DD` and `HH:MM`):** Calendar dates and times of day are deliberately stored as plain strings in MongoDB to prevent artificial timezone shifts and conversion errors, as specified in `DATA_MODEL.md`.
2. **`emp_code` as a business string:** `emp_code` is a customer-assigned immutable identifier (e.g. `EMP0001`), not MongoDB's internal `_id`. Keeping `_id` internal and hidden is by design.
3. **Legacy records omitting `history` or `half_day`:** Seeded legacy data may omit these fields; defaulting them to `[]` and `False` in application code is intentional and compliant with the data model.
4. **`$dateFromString` timezone set to UTC for `$dayOfWeek`:** The `date` field stores IST calendar dates as strings (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Parsing them as UTC midnight datetimes does not change the weekday, because the date component is preserved regardless of timezone.
